Selected Research Experience

Quantum Tomography

  • Co-led physically constrained, matrix-free quantum process reconstruction, published in TMLR.
  • Developed Bures-geometric flow matching for posterior quantum tomography, accepted to NeurIPS 2026.

Variational Transformer Ansatz for Quantum Many-Body Simulation

  • Designed a transformer representation of density operators for dissipative quantum steady states.
  • Implemented the scientific computing pipeline in JAX and NetKet and drafted the manuscript, published in Physical Review B.

Active Learning for 3D Molecular Graphs

  • Developed geometric uncertainty sampling for label-efficient molecular property prediction.
  • Implemented the sampling strategy, trained models, prepared figures, and drafted the manuscript, published at NeurIPS 2024.

Dataset Curation for Protein Spectroscopy

  • Curated iron-containing protein structures and X-ray absorption spectra into a machine-learning dataset.
  • Co-released XDIP and its curation notebooks; presented at the NeurIPS 2025 AI4Mat Workshop.

Adaptive Retrieval for Language Models

  • Designed uncertainty-based gating to selectively trigger retrieval in question-answering systems.
  • Implemented the retrieval pipeline with FAISS, sentence transformers, and Hugging Face models; published in TMLR.
